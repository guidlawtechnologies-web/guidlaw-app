RoadRight

Install the App

No app store needed — add it directly to your home screen

🌐 roadright.ca/driver
🍎

iPhone

Use Safari browser

1
Open Safari
Must be Safari — not Chrome or Firefox
2
Go to roadright.ca/driver
Type it into the Safari address bar
3
Tap the Share button
The box with an arrow at the bottom of the screen
⬆️ Share button — bottom centre of Safari
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Scroll down in the share menu to find it
➕ Add to Home Screen
5
Tap "Add"
RoadRight appears on your home screen — tap it to open
🤖

Android

Use Chrome browser

1
Open Chrome
Must be Chrome — not Samsung Browser or Firefox
2
Go to roadright.ca/driver
Type it into the Chrome address bar
3
Tap the three dots ⋮
Top right corner of Chrome
⋮ Menu — top right of Chrome
4
Tap "Add to Home Screen"
Or Chrome may show an install banner at the bottom automatically
➕ Add to Home Screen
5
Tap "Add"
RoadRight appears on your home screen — tap it to open
💡

Once installed, RoadRight opens full screen like a native app — no browser bar, no distractions. The big blue button connects you to a licensed lawyer or paralegal in seconds.
